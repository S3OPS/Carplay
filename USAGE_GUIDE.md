# CarPlay Screen Mirror - Usage Guide

A complete guide on how to use the CarPlay Screen Mirror app to stream content from your iPhone to your car's display.

## Quick Start

1. **Connect to CarPlay** (USB or wireless)
2. **Open the app** on your iPhone
3. **Tap "Start Mirroring"**
4. **Open any app** (Netflix, YouTube, Photos, etc.)
5. **View on your car screen** 🎉

## Detailed Instructions

### Initial Connection

#### Wired CarPlay Connection:

1. Start your car
2. Connect your iPhone to the car's USB port using a Lightning cable
3. Wait for the CarPlay interface to appear on your car's display
4. You should see the CarPlay home screen with available apps

#### Wireless CarPlay Connection:

1. Enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on your iPhone
2. Go to Settings → General → CarPlay
3. Select your car from "Available Cars"
4. Follow the pairing instructions on your car's screen
5. Once paired, CarPlay will connect automatically when you start your car

### Starting Screen Mirroring

#### Method 1: From Your iPhone

1. **Launch the App**:
   - Find "CarPlay Mirror" on your home screen
   - Tap to open

2. **Check Status**:
   - You'll see "Status: Ready" at the top
   - The "Start Mirroring" button should be green and enabled

3. **Start Mirroring**:
   - Tap the "Start Mirroring" button
   - If prompted, grant Screen Recording permission:
     - Tap "OK" on the permission dialog
     - The permission is only asked once

4. **Confirm Active**:
   - Status changes to "Status: Mirroring Active 🟢"
   - The "Stop Mirroring" button becomes enabled (red)
   - The "Start Mirroring" button becomes disabled

#### Method 2: From CarPlay Display

1. **Find the App**:
   - On your car's CarPlay display, look for "CarPlay Mirror"
   - Tap to open the app

2. **Select Option**:
   - You'll see a menu with two options
   - Tap "Start Screen Mirroring"

3. **Confirm Active**:
   - The car display shows "Mirroring Active"
   - The text turns green
   - Your phone also updates to show active status

### Using Screen Mirroring

Once mirroring is active, here's how to use it:

#### Watching Netflix:

```
1. Keep the CarPlay Mirror app in the background
2. Open the Netflix app on your iPhone
3. Browse and select content to watch
4. Tap the play button
5. The video appears on your car screen in real-time
6. Control playback from your iPhone:
   - Pause/Play
   - Seek through timeline
   - Volume adjustment
   - Subtitles/audio tracks
```

**Tips for Netflix**:
- Use landscape orientation for better viewing
- Ensure a stable CarPlay connection
- Start playback before driving (safety first!)

#### Watching YouTube:

### Controlling Playback

#### Volume Control:

1. **Using Phone**:
   - Use iPhone volume buttons
   - Or use on-screen app controls

2. **Using Car**:
   - Use car's volume knob/buttons
   - Usually synced with iPhone volume

#### Pause/Resume:

- **In App**: Use app's pause button
- **From Lock Screen**: Control center provides media controls
- **Voice**: "Hey Siri, pause" (when safe)

#### Navigation:

- All touch interactions happen on your iPhone
- The car display is view-only
- Navigate apps normally on your phone

### Stopping Screen Mirroring

#### From iPhone:

1. Return to the CarPlay Mirror app
2. Tap the "Stop Mirroring" button (red)
3. Status changes to "Status: Stopped"
4. "Start Mirroring" becomes available again

#### From CarPlay:

1. Open CarPlay Mirror on car display
2. Select "Stop Screen Mirroring"
3. Display shows "Mirroring Stopped"
4. Both interfaces update

#### Automatic Stop:

Mirroring automatically stops when:
- You disconnect from CarPlay
- You lock your phone for extended period
- Battery reaches critical level
- System needs to free resources

### Best Practices

#### For Best Quality:

1. **Good Connection**:
   - Use high-quality Lightning cable for wired
   - Stay close to car for wireless CarPlay
   - Avoid USB hubs or extensions

2. **Phone Position**:
   - Place phone in a stable position
   - Ensure good ventilation (avoid overheating)
   - Keep screen on for responsive mirroring

3. **App Settings**:
   - Set video apps to highest quality
   - Enable hardware acceleration if available
   - Close unnecessary background apps

#### For Battery Life:

1. **Charge While Using**:
   - Use car's USB port for charging
   - Or use a car charger
   - Mirroring is battery-intensive

2. **Optimize Settings**:
   - Lower screen brightness
   - Enable Low Power Mode if needed
   - Close unused apps

#### For Privacy:

1. **Be Aware**:
   - Everything on your screen is visible in the car
   - Notifications appear on car display
   - Consider Do Not Disturb mode

2. **Before Mirroring**:
   - Close sensitive apps
   - Clear notifications
   - Check what's on screen

### Troubleshooting During Use

#### Video Stuttering:

**Problem**: Choppy or lagging video

**Solutions**:
1. Check cable connection (if wired)
2. Move phone closer to car (if wireless)
3. Close other apps on iPhone
4. Restart mirroring
5. Reduce video quality in streaming app

#### Audio Out of Sync:

**Problem**: Audio doesn't match video

**Solutions**:
1. Pause and resume video
2. Restart mirroring
3. Check for iOS updates
4. Restart both phone and car system

#### Display Cut Off:

**Problem**: Edges of screen not visible

**Solutions**:
1. This is normal for some car displays
2. Rotate phone if applicable
3. Zoom out in the app you're using
4. Check car display settings for zoom/fit options

#### No Audio:

**Problem**: Video plays but no sound

**Solutions**:
1. Check car volume
2. Unmute iPhone
3. Check app's volume settings
4. Restart mirroring
5. Ensure audio source is set to CarPlay in car settings

### Advanced Features
